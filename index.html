Questions

Is JobVetter free?

Yes. Saving and tracking unlimited jobs, the scam check, follow-up reminders and your skills profile are free forever. Pro adds exact match scores, the Kanban board, the salary detector and AI writing.

Which browsers does it work on?

Microsoft Edge, Firefox and Chrome (Chrome Web Store listing coming soon).

Do you see my data?

No. There is no JobVetter server. Everything stays in your browser's extension storage.

Does the AI make things up?

JobVetter instructs the AI to use only facts from your resume and never invent jobs, degrees or skills. Always read the result before sending.
