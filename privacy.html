Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Short version: JobVetter has no account and no server. Everything you save stays in your own browser. We (the developer) never receive your jobs, skills, notes or resume.

1. What JobVetter stores

JobVetter stores the following only in your browser's local extension storage, on your device:

  • Jobs you save: title, company, location, link, job description, status, notes and the dates you saved or updated them.
  • Your skills list, and, if you add them, your name and resume text.
  • Your AI settings, including your AI provider API key, if you choose to use AI features.
  • Documents the AI writes for you (for example a cover letter) when you save them to a job.

This data is not sent to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service. You can delete it at any time by removing the extension, or delete individual jobs in the dashboard.

Browser sync. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on (for example a Firefox account), JobVetter also keeps a small copy of your saved jobs (without full descriptions), your skills, your name and your Pro license key in your browser's own sync storage, so they come back if you reinstall. This copy is stored by your browser's sync service under your account, never on a JobVetter server. Your AI key, resume and AI-written documents are never synced.

3. Why JobVetter needs its permissions

  • Storage: to save your jobs and settings on your device.
  • Active tab and scripting: to read the job details on the page you are viewing, only when you click the JobVetter button.
  • Access to job sites (such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Rozee.pk): to show the save button and the skills-match card on job pages. JobVetter reads only job details on these pages.
  • Access to AI provider websites (Google Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter): to send your AI requests when you use AI features.
  • Access to api.gumroad.com: to check your Pro license key.
  • Alarms: to re-check your Pro license every few days.

4. Children

JobVetter is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13.
